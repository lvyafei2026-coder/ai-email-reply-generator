Choosing the right tone

Professional — For clients, partners, and formal business communication. Neutral, clear, and respectful.
Friendly — For colleagues you know well, collaborators, and warm introductions. Sounds human, not corporate.
Concise — For busy recipients who want the answer, not the story. Three sentences or fewer.
Firm — For saying no, holding a boundary, or declining a request without sounding hostile.

Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?

Yes. It runs on Cloudflare Workers AI with a free daily quota. Normal use won't hit any limits.

Is my email content stored?

No. Your email is sent to the AI model for processing and discarded immediately after. Nothing is stored, logged, or used for training.

Will the reply sound like me?

Not perfectly. The tool produces a clean professional draft — you should edit it to match your voice and add any personal details. Treat it as a starting point, not a finished product.

Can I paste confidential emails?

We don't store your content, but the text is sent to a third-party AI model running on Cloudflare's infrastructure. For highly sensitive material — legal, medical, or financial — consider removing names and specifics before pasting.

What if the generated reply misses the point?

Try adding context in the "Additional context" field. For example: "The customer is asking for a refund, which we've agreed to." The model uses this to shape the reply. If it's still off, the original email may be too ambiguous for an AI to handle reliably.

Does it invent names or promises?

It uses only what you paste. Anything it could not know stays as a placeholder for you to fill in.

Can it handle a long thread?

Paste the most recent two or three messages. The reply is based on the newest question, not the whole history.

Will it sound like me?

Tone options get you close. Edit the greeting and sign-off to match your usual style.
